name: ui-design
description: Avoid these techniques when designing UI layout, colors, forms, etc.
---

# Colors
Do not use:
- a cream or off-white background
- a night or off-black/blue background
- a background on an element that is washed out by its surrounding or adjacent element background
  - Example: dark orange rgb(46, 26, 12) next to darker dark orange rgb(34, 19, 10)
- dark background with dark text color
- light background with light text color
- muted or faint colors
- font/icon colors that are washed out (faint, muted) by the background (not enough contrast)
- a lighter color for a nav bar/side panel/top nav than the main background color (the main background should be lighter, with darker colors on the panels/nav areas)

# Typography
Do not use:
- italic accent words in headings
- numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels
- font sizes only one pixel different from another on the page
- font sizes under 12px

# Forms and Buttons
Do not use:
- pill-shaped buttons
- different button styles
